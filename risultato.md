Musical artist
**TonyPitony** (born 12 April 1996) is an Italian singer-songwriter and performer known for his provocative and [satirical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satirical_music "Satirical music") songs blending [electronic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_music "Electronic music") and [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_R%26B "Contemporary R&B") atmospheres.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:0-2)
Active since 2020, he gained national prominence in 2025 following the viral success of several singles and live performances characterized by theatrical staging and controversial lyrics.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:1-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-4)
## Early and personal life
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=TonyPitony&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early and personal life")]
TonyPitony (whose real name has been alleged to be **Ettore Ballarino** or **Simone De Simone**) was born and raised in [Syracuse, Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse,_Sicily "Syracuse, Sicily").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-chimera-1)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-allmusic-6) After completing his secondary education at the _[liceo scientifico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liceo_scientifico "Liceo scientifico")_ Einaudi, he moved at the age of 19 to [Epsom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epsom "Epsom"), United Kingdom, where he received a scholarship in acting at [Laine Theatre Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laine_Theatre_Arts "Laine Theatre Arts") college. He remained in the UK for approximately seven years, working as a voice actor and appearing in small theatre productions in [London's West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London%27s_West_End "London's West End"), as well as performing in private shows for Scottish aristocratic audiences.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-chimera-1)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:5-7)
Following an unsuccessful audition for the musical _[Sister Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Act_\(musical\) "Sister Act \(musical\)")_ and the suspension of theatrical activities due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"), he began developing an independent artistic project under the pseudonym TonyPitony. Upon returning to Syracuse, he performed with the local band Sam & the Engineers, appearing on stage wearing an [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley") mask.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:5-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:2-8)
## Career
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=TonyPitony&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")]
###  2020–2024: _X Factor_ audition and first album
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=TonyPitony&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2020–2024: X Factor audition and first album")]
In July 2020, he independently released his debut studio album, _Nel 2067_ , which combined electronic and dance sounds with irreverent and comedic lyrics. Later that year, he auditioned for the [fourteenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_Factor_\(Italian_TV_series\)_season_14 "X Factor \(Italian TV series\) season 14") of _[X Factor Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_Factor_\(Italian_TV_series\) "X Factor \(Italian TV series\)")_ , performing a deliberately provocative [neomelodic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neomelodic_music "Neomelodic music")-style rendition of [Leonard Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Cohen "Leonard Cohen")'s "[Hallelujah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallelujah_\(Leonard_Cohen_song\) "Hallelujah \(Leonard Cohen song\)")". Although judge [Mika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mika_\(singer\) "Mika \(singer\)") praised his vocal abilities, he was eliminated during the audition phase and did not advance to the live shows.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:1-3)
During the early years of his career, his popularity remained largely local, with performances primarily at small festivals and community events. In 2023 he released several singles, including "Striscia" which leaned toward [indie pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_pop "Indie pop") influences, and "Donne ricche" and "Polietilene", which retained his earlier dance-oriented style. His visibility increased significantly through word-of-mouth and viral circulation on platforms such as [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:0-2)
###  2025–present
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=TonyPitony&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2025–present")]
In April 2025, he released his second studio album, _TonyPitony_ , also independently produced. After a modest initial reception, the album achieved wider commercial success thanks to the viral singles "Culo" and "Giovanni", both of which entered [Spotify Viral 50 chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify_Viral_50 "Spotify Viral 50") in Italy.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:2-8) Collaborations with online personalities, including YouTuber [Favij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Favij "Favij"), further expanded his audience.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-9)
Throughout 2025, TonyPitony performed at several Italian festivals and venues, including Poplar Festival in [Trento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trento "Trento"), Hiroshima Mon Amour in [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin "Turin"), Fabrique in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan "Milan"), and Estragon in [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna "Bologna"). In December 2025, following the success of an acoustic performance accompanied by guitarist [Marco Castello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Castello "Marco Castello").[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-10) he released the EP _Peccato per i testi_ , featuring five acoustic versions of previously released songs. The acoustic version of "Donne ricche" became his first top-ten single on the [FIMI Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_singles_chart "Italian singles chart"), peaking at number two.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:3-11) The album TonyPitony also entered the [FIMI Albums Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIMI_Albums_Chart "FIMI Albums Chart"), remaining for nine weeks and peaking at number nine.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:3-11)
In 2026, he was selected to perform the official theme song of the FantaSanremo game associated with the [Sanremo Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanremo_Music_Festival "Sanremo Music Festival"). He also appeared as a guest on [Fiorello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosario_Fiorello "Rosario Fiorello")'s television program _La pennicanza_[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-13) and was confirmed as a guest performer during the cover night of [Sanremo Music Festival 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanremo_Music_Festival_2026 "Sanremo Music Festival 2026") in a duet with [Ditonellapiaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditonellapiaga "Ditonellapiaga"), with the song "[The Lady Is a Tramp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lady_Is_a_Tramp "The Lady Is a Tramp")";[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:4-14) their performance ultimately won the night.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-15) A summer tour titled _Estatony_ was announced for 2026, with several dates selling out in advance.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:4-14) TonyPitony also collaborated with [Canova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Canova "Michele Canova") on the single "English Lesson Number One", set to be released on 13 March 2026.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-allmusic-6)
In early March 2026, an [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter") user tweeted a photo allegedly depicting TonyPitony without his mask. The image garnered quick circulation but also raised questions regarding a possible violation of the artist's privacy.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-allmusic-6)[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-16)
## Musical style and themes
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=TonyPitony&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Musical style and themes")]
TonyPitony's music incorporates elements of [comedy rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_rock "Comedy rock") alongside [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_R%26B "Contemporary R&B"), [electronic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_music "Electronic music"), and [indie pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_pop "Indie pop") songwriting. His production style is generally polished and aligned with contemporary mainstream trends.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:1-3)
His lyrics are known for their provocative, explicit, and often deliberately offensive content, including [misogynistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misogyny "Misogyny") and controversial stereotypes. In interviews, he has stated that this approach was partly intended to attract attention and generate public debate.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-17) His live performances are structured as theatrical shows combining music, comedy sketches, improvisation, and physical interaction with the audience.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-18)
Critical reception has been polarized. Some commentators have described his work as a sharp and unsettling reflection of contemporary [pop culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_culture "Pop culture"),[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-19) while others have criticized it as vulgar and regressive.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-:4-14) His lyrics have also been accused of referencing themes associated with [incel subculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incel "Incel").[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TonyPitony#cite_note-20)
